# Data-Visualization-Tableau

**Project Overview**

This project presents an analytical study of EdX, a prominent MOOC (Massive Open Online Course) provider, focusing on learner engagement and course performance for the academic year 2012-2013. The goal is to provide data-driven insights to assist directors in making informed decisions for the upcoming academic year.

**Objective**

As part of this study, I developed three interactive dashboards using Tableau that provide insights into:

EdX - Academic Business Report (Summary)
Total learners
Number of courses offered
Course completion rates
Course distribution among learners
EdX - Academic Business Report (Geographical)
Learner distribution across various geographical regions
EdX - Academic Business Report (Learners' Attributes)
Age distribution of learners
Qualification breakdown

**Tools & Technologies**

Data Analysis: SQL, Python (Pandas, NumPy)
Visualization: Tableau
Data Preparation: ETL, Data Cleaning


**Insights & Key Findings**

A significant percentage of learners did not complete their courses, highlighting potential engagement issues.
Courses from Harvard and MIT had varying completion rates, indicating differences in content difficulty or learner motivation.
The geographical distribution of learners revealed a higher concentration in North America and Europe, indicating potential markets for expansion.

**Future Enhancements**

Predictive Modeling: Implement machine learning models to forecast learner dropouts.
A/B Testing: Evaluate the impact of different course structures on completion rates.
Personalized Learning Paths: Use clustering techniques to recommend courses based on learner behavior.
